Mathematical model

Binary classification with logistic regression.

P(y=1|x) = 1 / (1 + e−(β₀+β·x))

Features are standardized using the training set only. The threshold converts probability into a class. The ROC curve evaluates multiple thresholds.

A real BCI requires protocols, individual calibration, clinical evaluation and risk management. This simulation only illustrates a processing pipeline.

The AI for Science cycle

AI expands the search space; experiments produce evidence.

01 · QUESTIONDesired function
02 · MODELDesign and prediction
03 · SELECTIONPriority candidates
04 · EXPERIMENTExperimental measurement
05 · ITERATIONUpdate the model and decide again

This laboratory models candidate selection and experimentation, not protein folding or real molecular interactions.

Mathematical model

Gaussian process + UCB acquisition.

UCB(x) = μ(x) + βσ(x) − λR(x)

μ(x): model-estimated activity.

σ(x): predictive uncertainty.

β: exploration–exploitation balance.

R(x): synthetic risk; λ controls its penalty.

The Gaussian process uses an RBF kernel over normalized descriptors. True activity remains hidden until the simulated experiment is run.
