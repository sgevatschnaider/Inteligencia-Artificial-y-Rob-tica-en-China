Latency and stopping distance

Explore how speed, response time and tire-road friction affect an idealized model. Compare prediction and result before changing each parameter.

PresentationSmart factoryModule guide

Model and units

v = speed / 3.6 in m/s. Response distance dᵣ = v × t. Braking distance dբ = v² / (2μg), with g = 9.81 m/s². Ideal stopping distance d = dᵣ + dբ. The chart shows the quadratic effect of speed and the linear effect of response time.

Instructions and questions

  1. Record the baseline result and predict what happens when speed doubles.
  2. Set response time to zero. Does braking distance disappear?
  3. Compare friction coefficients 0.7 and 0.2. Explain why faster software does not remove the physical limit.
  4. Export the results and justify every unit.

Assumptions: level road, constant deceleration μg after the response, no aerodynamics, slope, sensor failures or vehicle dynamics. This does not represent a specific ADAS system or determine safe real-world driving distances. Response time includes perception, decision and actuation.
