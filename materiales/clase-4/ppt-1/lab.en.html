4. Mathematical model

Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).

OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality

Availability (A): share of planned production time during which the bottleneck station is available.

Performance (P): total output relative to ideal capacity during available time.

Quality (Q): share of completed vehicles that meet quality requirements.

In this model, OEE is calculated for the station with the longest cycle time. It is an educational approximation of the line, not an audited metric from a real plant.

5. Questions to investigate

Use the scenarios to answer these questions with evidence from the simulation.

  1. What happens if we increase the speed of a station that is not the bottleneck?
  2. How much does output improve when predictive maintenance is introduced?
  3. What is the effect of reducing buffer capacity?
  4. Can OEE increase while good output falls?
