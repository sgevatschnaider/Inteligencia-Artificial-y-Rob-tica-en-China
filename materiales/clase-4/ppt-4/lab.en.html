How to interpret hydrogen

Physical output alone does not determine competitiveness.

kg H₂/day = (MW × 1,000 × 24 × utilization) / kWh per kg
Electricity cost = kWh/kg × USD/MWh / 1,000

The displayed cost adds electricity and other variable costs entered by the user. It excludes CAPEX, financing, compression, storage, transport, taxes and grid-connection costs.

Electrolysis is not automatically emissions-free: the input electricity determines much of its footprint.

Fusion: physical interpretation

Simplified triple product: density × temperature × energy confinement time.

nTτE = n × T × τE

The indicator is compared with an illustrative reference value of about 3 × 10²¹ keV·s·m⁻³ for a D-T reference condition. The exact criterion depends on the regime, composition, impurities and full energy balance.

Exceeding the indicator does not demonstrate ignition, net engineering gain or power-plant viability.
