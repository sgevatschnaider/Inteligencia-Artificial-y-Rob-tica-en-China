Illustrative model

Innovationt = Innovationt−1 × (1 + gI). Productivity and diffusion use distinct rates derived from the eight inputs; exposure combines chip, energy, trade and demand deficits.

There is no econometric fit, forecast error estimate, probability or identified causal relationship.

Equations and scope

t=0…19: from 2026 Q1=100 to 2030 Q4. There are twenty observations and nineteen quarterly intervals. Rates are quarterly assumptions rather than observed annual growth. Define r=(R&D−2.8)/3; h=(talent−65)/35; a=(adoption−58)/42; c=(chips−68)/32; e=(energy−74)/26; d=(demand−61)/39; x=(trade−65)/35; k=(coordination−67)/33.

Tension scores: chips=clamp[0.75(100−chips)+5;0;100]; energy=clamp[0.70(100−energy)+8;0;100]; trade=clamp[0.8(100−trade)+7;0;100]; demand=clamp[0.8(100−demand)+5;0;100]. Exposure=clamp[(0.32·chips+0.18·energy+0.30·trade+0.20·demand)(1+0.005t);0;100]. These four final variables are tension scores rather than original inputs.

The dashed baseline always retains all eight original settings. All five presets share the original equations without bonuses based on scenario names. Editing activates Custom; Restore preset returns Custom settings to Reference. Switching language retains parameters, quarter and theme.

A deterministic model without calibration, causal identification, probabilities or physical minimum constraints. Additive rates do not model multiplicative complementarities. It estimates neither GDP, empirical TFP, profitability nor future events. The 0–100 exposure score is not a probability. CSV includes inputs, rates, outputs and coefficients; SVG includes background and theme colours. Indices do not replace empirical evidence.
