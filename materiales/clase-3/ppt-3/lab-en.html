06 / Equations and scope

Front-end = wafers × dies per wafer × yield. Complete chips = min(front-end, packaging, HBM). Relative compute = complete chips × (network efficiency / 75%)^0.8, normalised against the initial reference.

Compound growth: front-end uses annual rate g, packaging uses 0.90 × g, and HBM uses 0.85 × g. Demand has its own rate and does not limit physical capacity. The supply/demand ratio can exceed 100%. Rates are teaching assumptions without company calibration.
