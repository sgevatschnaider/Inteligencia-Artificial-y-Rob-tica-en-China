06 / Mathematics and limits

Capacity = N × (28,800 s / effective cycle) × availability × dexterity × coordination. Processed = min(robot capacity, logistics, gross orders). Good output = processed × quality. Operating index = availability × dexterity × coordination × quality. It is a proxy, distinct from OEE measured against an ideal cycle.

With g = quarter/19: effective cycle = cycle × (1 − 0.17g). Perception gains 6 points; availability and coordination gain 3; quality gains 1.2. Caps of 99.5 and 99.7 apply. Dexterity = 0.65 + 0.35 × perception/100; effective coordination = 0.67 + 0.33 × coordination/100. Logistics = initial capacity × (1 + 0.14g). Orders = initial orders × 1.013^quarter. Queues, inventory, rework and physical paths are excluded.

Shift cost = 200 + 30 × robots + 0.04 × initial logistics capacity, in fictional monetary units. Unit cost = shift cost / good output. Coefficients are constant across all five presets. CAPEX, financing and taxes are excluded. This does not represent commercial prices or investment returns.
