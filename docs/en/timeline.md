# Timeline: AI and Robotics in China

**[Español](../es/cronologia.md) · [Back to the overview](../../README.en.md)**

This timeline selects policy, industrial, and technological milestones. A date records publication or entry into force; it does not imply uniform implementation across the country.

## Capacity building

- **2014:** the State Council issues its 2014–2020 planning outline for the social-credit system. The document covers government, commercial, social, and judicial trustworthiness; it does not establish a single national score for every citizen.
- **2015:** *Made in China 2025* is released. The industrial policy includes intelligent manufacturing, robotics, and advanced equipment.
- **2016:** the 13th Five-Year Plan and related programmes reinforce artificial intelligence, big data, and advanced manufacturing as strategic fields.
- **July 2017:** the State Council publishes the *New Generation Artificial Intelligence Development Plan*, setting phased goals through 2030.
- **2018–2020:** pilot zones, national open-innovation platforms, university programmes, and local funds expand. Implementation varies substantially by city and sector.

## Data, algorithms, and generative AI

- **November 2021:** the Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) enters into force.
- **March 2022:** the provisions governing algorithmic-recommendation services take effect.
- **January 2023:** the provisions governing deep-synthesis services take effect.
- **August 2023:** the interim measures for public-facing generative-AI services enter into force.
- **October 2023:** China presents its Global AI Governance Initiative.
- **2024:** foundation-model deployment, price competition, multimodal applications, and policy attention to embodied intelligence all accelerate.
- **January 2025:** DeepSeek-R1 intensifies international debate about reasoning, efficiency, open weights, and compute constraints.
- **March 2025:** measures for labelling AI-generated and synthetic content are issued.
- **August 2025:** the State Council publishes the “AI Plus” initiative, designed to integrate AI across economic and social sectors.

## Robotics and automation

- **2013:** China becomes the world's largest annual market for new industrial-robot installations.
- **2021:** China's operational stock exceeds one million industrial robots.
- **2023–2025:** Chinese manufacturers broaden their humanoid, quadruped, mobile-robot, and manipulation portfolios. Demonstrations improve rapidly, but sustained production and economic returns still need to be assessed use case by use case.
- **2024:** China installs 295,045 industrial robots—54% of the global total—and its operational stock exceeds two million.
- **2024:** Chinese suppliers account for 57% of industrial-robot sales in China, according to the IFR.

## How to read this timeline

National policy, technical availability, and productive adoption move at different speeds. A policy announcement is not implementation; a robot demonstration is not scaled production; and a model release does not by itself establish sustainable economic use.
