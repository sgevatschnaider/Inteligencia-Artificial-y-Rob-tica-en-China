# Sources and Methodology

Last reviewed: **26 August 2026**.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Primary material:** laws, regulations, plans, official statistics, technical papers, and product documentation.
2. **Comparable data:** specialist organisations such as the IFR and WIPO, and academic datasets with published methods.
3. **Independent research:** peer-reviewed articles, university centres, and analysis with identifiable sources.
4. **Specialist reporting:** useful for recent events, but checked against primary material when possible.
5. **Corporate releases:** valid evidence of announcements and claimed specifications; not proof of adoption or impact.

## Editorial rules

- Every material statistic states the year of the underlying data, not merely the publication year.
- Targets, budgets, announced investment, realised investment, revenue, and market size are treated as different concepts.
- “Open weights” is distinguished from “open source.”
- Deployments are described through accepted units, operation, customers, or other observable metrics when available.
- Chinese names use the most recognisable international form and, where useful, the Chinese name or pinyin.
- Translation prioritises conceptual equivalence over literal wording.
- Contested claims identify scope, evidence, and known limitations.
